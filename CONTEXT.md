# Second Lead

A learner brings a scene from a Mandarin show they love, plays one character aloud, and an AI plays the others. Support fades line by line until the learner can hold the scene, then the other characters start changing their lines.

## Language

### People and parts

**Learner**:
The person practicing. They speak one role in a scene.
_Avoid_: user, student, player

**Scene**:
A short stretch of dialogue between named characters, taken from one show.
_Avoid_: script, clip, episode, transcript (a transcript is raw input; a scene is what it becomes)

**Line**:
One character's turn in a scene: characters, pinyin and an English meaning.
_Avoid_: sentence, subtitle, utterance

**Role**:
The character the learner plays in a scene.
_Avoid_: part, persona

**Starter scene**:
An original scene that ships with the app, written in the style of a show, so anyone can try the app without importing.
_Avoid_: demo scene, sample

**Import**:
Turning a pasted transcript or a subtitle file into a scene. An imported scene belongs to the learner who imported it.

### Practice

**Take**:
One play-through of a scene by the learner, start to finish.
_Avoid_: session, run, attempt

**Hint**:
Help the learner asks for during a line: the meaning, slower audio, or a replay. Hints are counted.
_Avoid_: prompt (that word belongs to the AI), help, clue

**Check-in**:
The learner's one-tap rating of how a take felt, with an optional note.
_Avoid_: survey, feedback

**Research stats**:
Anonymous practice numbers (counts, rates, levels, timings; never words or notes) that a learner opts in to share.
_Avoid_: analytics, telemetry

**Transfer probe**:
A short unscripted dialogue outside the learner's scenes, used to check whether progress carries beyond the memorized scene.

**Support level**:
How much help a line shows, from full text with pinyin and English down to nothing. Each line has its own.
_Avoid_: difficulty, rung (unless naming one level)

**Pass**:
Saying a scripted line's words closely enough for it to fade. Tones are scored alongside but never block a pass.
_Avoid_: correct, success

**Fading**:
A line dropping to a lower support level after the learner says it well.

**Improv**:
A mode, unlocked when most lines are faded, in which the other characters vary their lines and the learner answers what was actually said.
_Avoid_: freestyle, free conversation

**Variation**:
A changed version of another character's line in improv. Variations come in three levels, unlocked in order:
- **Paraphrase**: same meaning, different words; the scripted reply still fits.
- **Swap**: a changed detail, so the scripted reply needs a matching change.
- **Branch**: a short detour off the script that steers back to it, needing new sentences.
_Avoid_: twist, curveball

**Tone tools**:
The ways a learner hears and sees tones: slowed or isolated playback, tone colors, record-and-compare, and the pitch contour overlay.
